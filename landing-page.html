The Process

Upload details about your pet—breed, age, weight, allergies, and health goals. Our AI learns what makes your companion thrive.

Collect Your Kitchen Waste

Save your food scraps in our collection container. Vegetables, fruits, grains, and more. Just what's safe for pets, of course.

Receive Custom Bars

We craft nutrient-dense bars using your waste and personalized recipes. Monthly deliveries, formula adjusted as your pet ages.
